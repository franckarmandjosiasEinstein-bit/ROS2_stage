\caption{Colour-vision crate detection (\phaseA{},
\filename{vision.py}). Everything derivable from the fixed camera
declaration is computed once in the initialisation band, so the per-frame
cost is one vectorised threshold, one flood fill and a handful of
$3\times3$ products. The two red branches are the defects found by
testing against supervisor ground truth rather than by inspection: a
projection that is geometrically unreliable, and a detection that the
lidar contradicts. The pose entering the projection is the robot's
\emph{own} estimate, never the simulator's --- which is why localisation
error shows up honestly as fruit position error.}
